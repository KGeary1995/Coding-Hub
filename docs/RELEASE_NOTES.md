## What's new

1.11.8 makes channels from your other computer and your accounts easier to tell apart:

- **Another computer's channels fill their tiles.** Looking at your Mac's channels from your PC (or the other way round), each one was drawn at the size of its tile on the Mac, small in the PC's bigger tiles. While you watch them and nobody is using the Mac, the Mac now redraws them at the PC tile's size. When you use the Mac again, it takes its own size back. Update both computers.
- **See which account a channel runs on.** Every Claude Code channel shows its account's name, such as Personal or Work, in that account's colour, with a stripe of the same colour down the tile's edge. A channel on your default login is named after the profile signed in to the same account, or shows the account's email. The phone and your other computers show the same names and colours. To tell accounts apart, Trace reads only the email and organization Claude Code keeps beside its settings.

## Install

- **Windows:** download `Trace-<version>-Windows-x64-Setup.exe` and run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognized app because the installer is not code-signed yet: choose **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** download the `.dmg` for your Mac (Apple menu → About This Mac shows Chip: Apple = arm64, Intel = x64), open it and drag the app to Applications.

Your settings, history, rules and memory stay in `~/.coding-hub` and carry over between versions.
